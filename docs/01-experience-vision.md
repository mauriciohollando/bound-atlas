# 01 — Experience Vision

## Working title (experience)

**The Bound Atlas**  
*(site / interactive GN name; prose may keep The Bound Maps)*

## One-sentence thesis

The site is a **Bound atlas that can be unpicked**: the visitor reads a sanctioned story-map, optionally explores soft edges, and watches their personal atlas rewrite as clues rhyme west with east.

## What “done” feels like

After Book 1, a visitor should feel they have:

1. **Lived a journey** (Meridian → Hollowford), not only read about one.  
2. **Held cartography as UI** — seals, folios, twin curves, wrong stars are tools and mysteries.  
3. **Owned time** — never trapped in a cutscene they can’t pace.  
4. **Earned a redrawn map** — Soft Middle veins visible on their hub atlas that weren’t there at ENTER.  
5. **Left wanting a second fire** — west to Crowe or deeper into Ashari patience (Book 2 hook).

## Non-negotiables

1. **Reader owns time.** Motion and score react to progress; they do not hijack it (Upgrade Soul / Daniels manifesto).  
2. **Cartography is the interface.** Prefer diegetic maps, seals, crows, posters over generic menus.  
3. **Primary grammar = scroll comic.** Other forms are set-piece islands.  
4. **Story flags gate spoilers.** Soft Middle toys cannot reveal letter/Twin Charts before Ch15/16 beats.  
5. **Progressive enhancement.** Beauty on high-end WebGPU; readable comic on modest mobile.  
6. **Image carries weight.** Text is lean; environment, sound, and panel design do emotional labor (Boat).  
7. **Personality over chrome.** Threshold ritual (ENTER), quiet UI (Shining), place density (Wormworld).

## Negotiables

- Full 3D hub vs illustrated 2.5D atlas  
- Autoscroll as optional assist  
- How game-like collectibles feel (light ARG vs pure comic extras)  
- Exact art mix (clear-line characters on painterly grounds is preferred, not locked)

## Emotional north stars by act

| Act | Chapters | Feeling |
|-----|----------|---------|
| I Call | 1–2 | Warmth, pomp, sealed warmth, invitation |
| II Crossing | 3–7 | Wonder → unease → beautiful trap |
| III Cost | 8–13 | Smoke, height, myth without lecture |
| IV Hinge | 14–17 | Homecoming, rupture, weather in the teeth |

## Success metrics (qualitative)

- A visitor can retell the Soft Middle in their own words after finishing.  
- Someone can “play” the hub for 10 minutes without reading and still feel Meridian ≠ Haven ≠ Hollowford.  
- Optional paths feel like vault side-corridors, not wikis.  
- Replaying unlocks noticing (Boat side trips, poster rumors, seal inspector).

## Anti-goals

- Feature soup (Boat + Exodus + Shining + full game competing on every page).  
- Encyclopedia dump disguised as exploration.  
- Autoplaying VO / video that steals panel time.  
- Inventory grind that breaks comic immersion.  
- Spoiling Ascension as a tooltip.

## Relationship to prose

Prose chapters remain **canon spine**.  
The site is an **instrumented performance** of that spine: same beats, different senses, optional depth.

## Tagline options

- *Read the sanctioned map. Notice where it lies.*  
- *The lines have held. For now.*  
- *When two lines sing, the middle wakes.*

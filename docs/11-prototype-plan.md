# 11 — Prototype Plan

Build **three spikes** before full Book 1 production. Each spike answers a risk.

---

## Spike A — Embodied chapter scroll (Boat grammar)

**Goal:** Prove vertical comic + reactive audio + one environmental sync.

**Content:** Abbreviated **Chapter 1** (graduation morning → seal warmth).  
**Includes:** Banner parallax, seal ritual (2D or light 3D), cinnamon/bell stem, Continue save.  
**Success:** Strangers finish in ~8–12 min and describe *feeling* of seal warmth without UI tooltip.  
**Fail if:** Feels like a blog with GIFs.

---

## Spike B — Journey panorama (Exodus grammar)

**Goal:** Prove horizontal/tilt ribbon doesn’t fight site IA.

**Content:** Compressed **Span Express** approach + mid-Span folio beat (no full mystery).  
**Includes:** Parallax pylons, pencil-refuse interaction, Vesper late smile, folio_partial flag.  
**Success:** Users orient geographically (“we left the New Continent”).  
**Fail if:** Motion sickness or “can’t find the story.”

---

## Spike C — Atlas hub + clue toy (CV + Soft Middle)

**Goal:** Prove hub personality + gated tool.

**Content:** Hub with Meridian + Saltmere + Span pins; fake flags toggle; seal inspector with live vs retired; locked twin overlay.  
**Includes:** ENTER ritual, pin sensory one-liners, Soft Middle vein hidden until toggle.  
**Success:** Feels like *your* gamified CV energy but diegetic to Bound Maps.  
**Fail if:** Feels like a game inventory bolted onto art.

---

## Order

1. A (story trust)  
2. C (atlas identity)  
3. B (journey spectacle)  

Then integrate A+C into a vertical slice: ENTER → Hub → Ch1 → Hub redraw.

## Prototype non-goals

- Full Ch1–17 art  
- Account system  
- Perfect Ashari conlang  
- Melk mountain final FX  

## Review checklist after spikes

- [ ] Reader time respected  
- [ ] Cartography-as-UI readable  
- [ ] Mobile Tier B readable  
- [ ] Flag gate works  
- [ ] Want to fund full chapter art

# 02 — Inspiration & References

Research synthesis for *The Bound Atlas*. Steal techniques, not skins.

---

## The Boat — [sbs.com.au/theboat](https://www.sbs.com.au/theboat/)

**What it is:** SBS interactive GN (Nam Le / Matt Huynh / Sam Petty). Vertical scroll, ink + WebGL, reactive soundscape, optional autoscroll, side paths, archival media.

**Steal:**
- Scroll-synced environment (waves ↔ our ink-rain, Span shimmer, mountain wind)
- Reader pace + optional autoscroll that respects density
- Side branches that deepen without derailing spine
- Text cut hard; image/sound carry mood
- Archival/evidence layer inside fiction
- Haptics where meaningful (rare, diegetic)

**Bound Maps mapping:**
- Ch1 courtyard sway of banners  
- Ch5 Span spray + sealed-light shimmer  
- Ch8 mountain “birth” screen-shake  
- Optional “vault archive” branches for Map Law / posters / crow notes  

**Avoid:** Treating every chapter as max-FX; Boat’s power is restraint between storms.

---

## Mauricio Hollando portfolio — [mauriciohollando.com](https://mauriciohollando.com)

**What it is:** Portal entry into a canvas-led, gamified / experiential CV — information as space you move through.

**Steal:**
- Threshold ritual (`ENTER`) before content
- Progress via exploration, not bullet lists
- Personality encoded in motion and rules
- Hub that teaches systems by walking them

**Bound Maps mapping:**
- Site boot: ink-dot / seal glow → ENTER the atlas  
- Hub map unlocks like “levels” (Meridian, Saltmere, Haven…)  
- “Skills” become seals learned, rivers surveyed, clues overlaid  

**Avoid:** Making the story hub feel like a résumé with dragons.

---

## Shining — [shining.302chanwoo.com](https://shining.302chanwoo.com/)

**What it is:** Short 3D storybook (Three.js / WebGPU). Pages, light, spatial transitions; almost no conventional UI.

**Steal:**
- Page-as-place; turn = spatial event
- Light as narrative grammar
- Illustration-first 3D (not tech demo)
- Quiet interaction; scene knows how to turn
- Honest capability gating + fallbacks

**Bound Maps mapping:**
- Open Guild seal (Ch1) as page-space  
- Temple basin (Ch12) as light volume  
- Crowe letter open (Ch15) as intimate book ritual  
- Compact “map-book” UI metaphor for chapter select  

**Avoid:** Requiring WebGPU for the whole comic; islands only.

---

## Exodus — Submarine Channel / Jasper Rietman

**What it is:** Motion comic; ~12m horizontal panorama; tilt/pan; 2.5D parallax; subtle loops; score.

**Steal:**
- Journey as continuous ribbon drawing
- Device-as-camera
- Clear-line readability at motion speed
- Parallax depth without full world sim
- Exhibition potential (physical panorama)

**Bound Maps mapping:**
- Ch3 road ribbon (wheat → citrus → sea)  
- Ch5 Span Express as hero panorama  
- Ch13 descent ribbon (cloud → forest → Kessari dust)  
- Optional “install” mode: long image + scroll/tilt  

**Avoid:** Horizontal-only site; use as chapter islands.

---

## Upgrade Soul — Ezra Claytan Daniels / Erik Loyer / Alexis Gideon

**What it is:** Immersive digital comic; 3D picture-box panels; score performed by reader swipe pace.

**Steal:**
- **Never take temporal control from the reader** (manifesto)
- Reactive music tied to panel/scroll progress
- Screen-native panel design first
- Depth effects inside comic grammar
- Unlockable extras after trust is earned

**Bound Maps mapping:**
- Panel advances cue Meridian bells / Soft Middle dissonance  
- Diorama panels for vault crates, monorail cars, ford stones  
- Extras: art of Melk’s ridge, Vesper’s page, seal inspector  

**Avoid:** Autoplay VO; “cinematic” mode that traps scroll.

**Read:** [Digital Comics Manifesto](https://screendiver.com/digital-comics-manifesto/)

---

## Wormworld Saga — Daniel Lieske

**What it is:** Painterly digital GN; infinite-canvas vertical chapters; overwhelming sense of place.

**Steal:**
- Extended establishing shots that engulf panels
- Scroll-as-camera (pans, fades, chases)
- Place density over UI
- Chapter = destination on a larger world
- Spatial previs for complex environments

**Bound Maps mapping:**
- Auric Haven plaza establish that becomes panel field  
- Spine of Eld climb as continuous vertical canvas  
- Hollowford two-bed river as living establish  
- Each chapter pin on hub returns you into that scroll  

**Avoid:** Beautiful empty tourism with no story pressure.
